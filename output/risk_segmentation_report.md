# Risk segmentation report

Generated by `python -m src.predict`. All numbers were computed by that run.

## 1. How the probabilities were produced
- 7,043 customers scored with 5-fold out-of-fold predictions (stratified, seed 42): each customer's probability and explanation come from a model fitted on the other four folds, with the hyperparameters selected on the training split.
- Out-of-fold quality over all customers: ROC-AUC 0.8455, PR-AUC 0.6525, Brier score 0.1356. These are cross-validation figures, not held-out test results (those are in `output/model_report.md`).
- 1,869 customers (26.5%) are already churned and 5,174 are active. Risk counts and revenue at risk below use ACTIVE customers only; churned customers cannot be retained.

## 2. Are the 0.30 / 0.70 tiers justified?
The thresholds are practical project assumptions from the brief, not universal truths. **Rule, fixed before looking at results:** keep them if the observed churn rate of each tier falls inside that tier's probability band (so a 'High' customer really is more likely than not to churn, and so on).

| tier | band | customers | mean_predicted | observed_churn_rate | ci_low | ci_high | observed_inside_band | predicted_inside_ci |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | 0.00 to 0.30 | 4353 | 0.103 | 0.095 | 0.086 | 0.104 | True | True |
| Medium | 0.30 to 0.70 | 2328 | 0.489 | 0.498 | 0.478 | 0.519 | True | True |
| High | 0.70 to 1.00 | 362 | 0.773 | 0.820 | 0.778 | 0.857 | True | False |

**Outcome:** observed rate inside the band for every tier: **True**. The default thresholds are kept.
`predicted_inside_ci` shows whether the tier's mean predicted probability lies inside the 95% Wilson interval of its observed rate (calibration within the tier).

## 6. Caveats
- The risk tiers describe predicted probability, not a recommended action; a retention programme would also weigh offer cost and success rate, which the dataset does not contain.
- 'High value' uses monthly charges only. A customer's real value also depends on margin and expected remaining lifetime, which are unknown here.
- Because the monthly bill is determined by the services held, 'high value' customers are largely those with fiber and several services.
- Drivers (`TopRiskDriver1-3`) are the model's associations for that customer, not causes of churn.
- The saved model (`models/churn_model.pkl`) is fitted on the 80% training split; new customers are scored with it, not with the out-of-fold models.
