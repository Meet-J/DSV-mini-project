## 5. Caveats
- Effects are conditional on the other features. A feature can look protective or harmful here while showing the opposite in a one-variable EDA comparison, because features overlap.
- Since the monthly bill is determined by the services (see the feature-selection section of the notebook), each service column now carries both 'has the service' and 'pays for it'; this dataset cannot separate the two.
- The model treats 'No internet service' and 'No phone service' as 'No' for each add-on, so those levels share one input value; their different average effects in the level table reflect interactions with other features (mainly InternetService).
- Importance and contributions use the held-out test set for explanation only; nothing was changed because of them.
- A statistical association in one snapshot is not evidence that changing a feature would change churn.
